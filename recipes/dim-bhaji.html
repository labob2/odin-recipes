Dim Bhaji Recipe

Dim Bhaji

Dim Bhaji (or Bengali Omelette) is a beloved, spiced egg dish from the Indian subcontinent. Whisk eggs with thinly sliced red onions, green chiles, cilantro, and salt, then fry in hot oil until golden brown. It's typically served with rice, roti, or parathas.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Onions
  • Green chili
  • Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Break eggs and put onions and chili in them
  2. Put oil in pan and put the mixture in
  3. Flip it half way cooked and voila
