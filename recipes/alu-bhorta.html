Alu Bhorta Recipe

Alu Bhorta

Alu bhorta is a traditional Bengali comfort food made from boiled, mashed potatoes mixed with pungent mustard oil, onions, and fiery chilies. It is a simple, highly flavorful dish popular in Bangladesh and West Bengal.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Onions
  • Dried chili
  • Mustard oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Peel and boil the potatoes
  2. Mix up the chili, salt, and diced onions
  3. Add mustard oil to liking and mash it all up. Do salt checks
